# Synthetic reporting standards policy

**Fictional demo policy — effective 2024-01-01**

This policy describes the sample business dataset used by the Business Analyst
AI demonstration. It is not a real company's accounting policy.

Revenue analyses include completed orders only. Each order item contributes
its quantity multiplied by its unit price in integer cents. Returned and
cancelled orders are not completed sales. Reports should name the period and
metric being compared, preserve the units returned by the analysis, and
distinguish measured results from explanations mentioned in sample documents.

Management observations in this synthetic corpus are not proof of causation.
When a document suggests a possible reason for a change, report it as an
attributed observation and retain the evidence caveat.
