# Returns and damaged products

**Synthetic company policy — effective 2024-01-01**

Customers should report damaged products within 14 calendar days of delivery.
Support staff should request the order number and a description of the damage.
Photographs may be requested when they help assess the reported issue.

After review, support may offer a replacement or refund under the applicable
consumer terms. A returned order is not counted as completed sales in the
business reporting dataset. This policy does not establish why sales changed.
