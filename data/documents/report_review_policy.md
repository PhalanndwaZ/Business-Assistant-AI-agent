# Synthetic report review policy

**Fictional demo policy — effective 2024-01-01**

Reports produced for this demonstration should be based on validated analysis
results and cited document passages. Reviewers should check that each numeric
claim matches its analysis result and that each document-derived claim is
supported by retrieved source text.

If evidence is incomplete, retain a caveat rather than presenting an
unsupported conclusion. Descriptions of possible causes must remain attributed
to their source and must not be presented as established causation.

This sample policy documents the demonstration workflow only. It is not a
real organization's reporting or approval procedure.
